DETERMINATION OF FERMI ENERGY

AIM

To determine the Fermi energy and Fermi temperature of a metal wire.

APPARATUS

Heating setup, copper wire, thermometer, and multimeter.

CIRCUIT DIAGRAM

NATURE OF GRAPH

FORMULAE

(a)  EF = [ (n e2 π A r2) / (L sqrt(2m)) ]2 × (ΔR / ΔT)2

Where         EF is the Fermi energy of the material of the given coil in J

n is the number density of electrons in m-3

m is the mass of electron in kg

e is the charge of the electron in C

A is λF × T

λF is the mean free path of electrons in m

T is the reference temperature of the wire in K

r is the radius of the copper wire in m

L is the length of the copper wire in m

ΔR / ΔT is the slope of the straight line obtained by plotting resistance of the metal against absolute temperature of the metal in ΩK-1

Also, Fermi Energy can be written as,  EF = C (T / R)2 (ΔR / ΔT)2

Where, C = constant = 11.22 × 10-19 J

R is resistance at reference temperature T

(b)  TF = EF / k

Where,         TF is the Fermi temperature in K

K = Boltzmann constant = 1.38 × 10-23 J/K,

EF is Fermi energy in J
