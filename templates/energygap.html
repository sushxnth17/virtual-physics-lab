Experiment: 8

ENERGY GAP OF THE SEMICONDUCTOR

AIM

To determine the energy gap of the semiconductor (thermistor).

APPARATUS REQUIRED

Multimeter, thermistor, heater, thermometer, and beaker.

FORMULA USED

$$E_g = \frac{2.303 \times 2K \times S}{1.601 \times 10^{-19}}\ \text{eV}$$

Where

Eg = Energy gap of the given semiconductor in eV

K = Boltzmann constant = 1.38 × 10-23 JK-1

S = Slope of the graph

THEORY

Thermistors are semiconductor devices whose resistance varies with temperature. The variation of resistance (R) with absolute temperature (T) is given by:

$$R = R_0 e^{\left(E_g / 2KT\right)}$$

Taking logarithm on both sides:

$$\log R = \log R_0 + \frac{E_g}{2KT}$$

Therefore, a plot of log R versus 1/T is a straight line. If the slope of this graph is S, the energy gap can be determined using the above formula.

PROCEDURE

  1. An ohmmeter is connected across the thermistor (red to red and black to black).
  2. The thermistor is immersed in hot water, heated to about 95°C.
  3. During cooling, the resistance of the thermistor is noted for different temperatures at 5°C intervals.
  4. The readings are tabulated and a graph of log R versus 1/T is plotted. The slope (S) of the straight line is determined.
  5. The energy gap of the given semiconductor is calculated using the formula.
