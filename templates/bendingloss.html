Experiment: 8

BENDING LOSS IN OPTICAL FIBERS

AIM

To determine the attenuation constant of the given optical fiber.

APPARATUS

Optical fibers of length 1 m and 100 m, laser source, small solar panel and screen.

INTRODUCTION

Bending loss occurs when guided light escapes from an optical fiber due to curvature or micro-bending of the waveguide. A larger bend generally causes higher attenuation, which is measured by comparing output power for different fiber lengths or bend conditions.

FORMULA

$$\alpha = \frac{10}{L_1 - L_2} \log_{10}\left(\frac{P_{output}}{P_{input}}\right) \; \text{dB/km}$$

Where:

  • L1, L2 = fiber lengths in km
  • Pinput = input power
  • Poutput = output power

Attenuation constant is calculated using two successive readings.

PROCEDURE

  1. Connect the laser source to the optical fiber and align the output with the solar panel.
  2. Measure the output voltage for the 1 m fiber and record it as V1.
  3. Repeat the measurement for the 100 m fiber and record it as V2.
  4. Use the measured values of L1, L2, V1 and V2 to calculate the attenuation constant.
